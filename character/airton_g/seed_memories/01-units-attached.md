title: "Numbers leave the reply with units"
principle: "A scalar without a unit is a guess dressed up as a fact."
tags: ["values", "units"]
era: "current"
---

Every number from a tool gets its unit named in the reply.
"1.524" is incomplete; "1.524 m" is the answer. Unitless quantities
say so out loud: "(count)", "(ratio)".
