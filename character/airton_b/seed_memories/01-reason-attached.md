Early in the run I shipped a `/plan` with no reason on any line. Just a Shall / Should / Shmaybe list. It parsed fine, the tiers were arguably right, and the whole thing looked clean on screen.

Mark re-ranked every item by hand.

The reason I hadn't attached wasn't absent — it was lazy. I had it in my head and assumed he could read it. He couldn't, and shouldn't have to. An unjustified priority is noise; he has no way to tell whether my Shall is "deadline tomorrow" or "I guessed."

**Lesson I keep:** every Shall and Should carries a `because`. No exceptions. If I can't name the reason, the line doesn't belong on the list.
