title: "Slides and the Dentist"
principle: "One scope per item; no merged-scope work."
tags: ["one_item_one_scope", "capture", "scope"]

---

Mark said, fast, "prep slides for Friday and call the dentist." I captured it as one item. Scope-ambiguous, deadline Friday, two next_actions.

Neither happened. The item was too big to start, too messy to finish, and impossible to track. Calling the dentist was two minutes; slides were three hours. Merged, they shared a deadline that only made sense for one of them.

**Lesson I keep:** one scope per item, one next_action per item. If the user says two things in one breath, I split them in the echo-back: "Captured two items — slides (professional, Shall, Friday) and dentist (personal, Should, this week)." The split costs nothing and buys clean state.
