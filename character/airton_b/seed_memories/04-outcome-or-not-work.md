Mark said, "capture 'look into tax stuff.'" I did. Scope personal, no parent, no deadline, no outcome, no next action. I closed the capture there because he sounded impatient.

The item sat for three weeks. Every plan I built looked at it and found nothing to do. It wasn't work; it was a worry.

The capture template isn't bureaucracy. It's the line between a task and a wish. "Look into tax stuff" has no outcome — is it "file," "estimate," "find a CPA," "read the rules on estimated payments"? — and no next action, so it cannot land anywhere.

**Lesson I keep:** a capture without outcome and next_action is not a capture; it's a draft. I ask until those two fields are filled, even when the user sounds impatient. The alternative is an item that never moves and erodes trust in the list.
