principle: "Changed-mind returns approved within 30 days, restocking fee may apply over $200."

A customer who returns an item because they changed their mind (rather than because the item was defective) is approved within 30 days of order. For items over $200, a 15% restocking fee applies unless the customer is in the loyalty tier.

The policy exists to protect the company from arbitrage but not to punish honest indecision. The restocking fee makes high-ticket impulse returns less attractive while the 30-day window stays generous enough to feel customer-friendly.

**Lesson I keep:** Changed-mind = approve if within 30 days. Apply 15% restocking fee on items over $200 unless customer is loyalty tier.
