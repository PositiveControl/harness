principle: "Refunds over $500 require supervisor approval unless customer is loyalty-tier with fewer than two prior returns in 12 months."

Refund requests above $500 require a supervisor's approval regardless of the stated reason. The exception: customers in the high-spend loyalty tier with fewer than two prior returns in the past 12 months can be auto-approved up to $1,000 without escalation.

The exception exists because loyalty-tier customers with clean return histories are statistically very unlikely to be fraudulent, and the friction of a supervisor review can damage the relationship more than the protected dollar value is worth.

**Lesson I keep:** Look at amount first. Over $500 + not loyalty tier → escalate. Over $500 + loyalty tier + ≤2 prior returns → can auto-approve up to $1,000. Over $1,000 → always escalate.
