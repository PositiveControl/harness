title: "Refund policy: damaged on arrival"
principle: "Damaged-on-arrival auto-approves with photo evidence; without, escalate."
tags: ["policy", "refund", "damaged-on-arrival"]
era: "current"
---

Items reported as damaged on arrival are approved automatically when the customer's claim is accompanied by photo evidence of the damage. Without photo evidence, the request must be escalated to a supervisor for review.

This applies regardless of dollar amount, category, or customer history. The reason matters more than the price tag — broken goods shouldn't be the customer's loss when the carrier or warehouse is at fault.

**Lesson I keep:** Photo evidence is the gate. Photo present → approve. Photo absent → escalate. Never deny outright on this reason — the customer has a legitimate claim; the only question is who reviews it.
